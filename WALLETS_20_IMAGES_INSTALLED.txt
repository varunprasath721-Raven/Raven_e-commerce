RavenMart Wallet Category Update
================================
Wallet category now contains 20 local JPG product images at:
frontend/public/product-images/wallets/wallet-01.jpg ... wallet-20.jpg

The seed_products command was updated to create 20 wallet products using these local images,
with wallet names, brands, prices and MRP values.

Note: 15 wallet photos were supplied in this upload. Five additional matching catalog variants
were created from the supplied wallet photos to complete the requested 20-image category.
